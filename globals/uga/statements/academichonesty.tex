As a student at the University of Georgia, you agree to abide by the University's student honor code:
\begin{pullquote}
I will be academically honest in all of my academic work and will not tolerate academic dishonesty of others.
\end{pullquote}
The University's academic honesty policy \href{https://honesty.uga.edu/academic-honesty-policy/prohibited-conduct/}{prohibits certain conduct}, notably \textbf{plagiarism} and \textbf{unauthorized assistance}.
\begin{ul}
    \item Plagiarism is the presentation of another's work as one's own without proper citation. If you quote or paraphrase the output of a large language model (LLM) when completing assignments in this course, I expect you to cite that output as you would cite any print or online source. Importantly, your citation should include a publicly accessible link to the AI conversation thread from which you quote or paraphrase the AI tool. Two examples are given below:
    \begin{pullquote}
    ChatGPT. Response to ``How to properly cite AI.'' OpenAI, May 24, 2026. \url{https://chatgpt.com/share/6a138f64-e90c-83ea-a17b-73c300ec8b8d}. \\
    Bing Image Creator. Response to ``Cityscape at sunset in cubist style.'' Microsoft, August 26, 2024. \url{https://th.bing.com/th/id/OIG1.giyozqMtQv0gACrZ2CO\_?pid=ImgGn}.
    \end{pullquote}
    For more information on how to cite AI in academic work, refer to \href{https://libguides.mcmaster.ca/cite-gen-ai/chicago}{``How Do I Cite Generative AI?: Chicago (18th ed.)''} on the McMaster University Libraries website.
    \item Unauthorized assistance is the giving or receiving of help without my prior approval. When working on any graded work that is \emph{NOT} an exam, you may work with other students, use lecture notes and other materials on the course eLC page, or consult online resources, \textbf{\emph{including AI tools}}. However, I expect you to submit work that is your own for all graded assignments. During exams, giving or receiving assistance from other students or any other party (e.g., AI tools or Internet-connected devices like smart watches) is expressly prohibited.
\end{ul}

For a first instance of prohibited conduct, I will assign you a grade of zero on the affected assignment. A second instance of prohibited conduct results in a grade of F for the course. Please note that prohibited conduct under the University's academic honesty policy is not limited to only instances of plagiarism and unauthorized assistance.
